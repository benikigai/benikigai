> I build AI systems for messy physical-world operations: commerce, factories, guest ops, and owner-led businesses where the software has to survive contact with real work.

I spent about a decade shipping consumer hardware from prototype to mass production at Apple and Meta. Since August 2026 I run Injester full time. I also build agentic infrastructure against live operating surfaces: a five-property short-term-rental business, a self-hosted property management stack, agent discovery protocols, and a home inference fleet.

More at **[benjaminshyong.com](https://benjaminshyong.com)** (it also answers questions, and speaks MCP).

## Currently building

- **[Injester](https://injester.com)**: turns AI shopping agents into customers. Reads a merchant's store, finds the facts an agent can't confirm, helps fix them, and retests.
- **[N+1 Labs](https://nplus1labs.com)**: simulation checked against real results. First product, Factory N+1: prove the next revision of a production line before you build it.
- **[Elias](https://github.com/benikigai/elias)**: my OpenClaw assistant. Seven agents, five channels, versioned memory, local inference.
- **[TurnkeyHost](https://preview.turnkeyhost.ai)**: the self-hosted PMS running five short-term rentals (reservations, guest comms, locks, cleaning, pricing, direct booking).
- **[Agent Web Protocol](https://agentwebprotocol.org)** ([spec](https://github.com/agentwebprotocol/spec)): an open discovery spec for what agents can do on a web surface.
- **[Stay Ikigai](https://stayikigai.com)**: the direct-booking STR brand and live testbed for all of the above.

## Hardware and operations

Systems and industrial engineer by training (Purdue SIE, CPIM). Supply chain and ERP at Lincoln Electric across Cleveland, Torreon and Shanghai. Six years at Apple: iPad Pro NPI, iPhone and AirTag accessories, and a special-project factory bring-up. Then Staff TPM for manufacturing at Meta, owning factory readiness for the Ray-Ban Display AR glasses from prototype through mass production.

The through-line is operational reality: mechanical, electrical, firmware, supply chain, yield, throughput, people and deadlines in the same room.
